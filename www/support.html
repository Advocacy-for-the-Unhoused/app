Common questions

How do I sign in?

Use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. The first time you sign in, choose your branch to finish registering.

My branch isn't listed. What do I do?

You can start a new branch during registration. You'll become its Branch President and can invite other volunteers to join.

How do I log volunteer hours?

From the Home screen, tap Hours, pick the event, and enter your time. All your past entries are under My History.

How do I record a donation?

Tap Record donations, then scan the slip's barcode with your camera or type the slip number, and enter the amount. The app never handles money. It only keeps a record of cash you collected in person.

How do I get reimbursed for supplies?

Open Reimbursement, describe what you bought, enter the amount, and attach a photo of the receipt. You can follow the request's status under My Expense Requests.

I'm not getting notifications.

Go to Settings → Notifications → AFU on your iPhone and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Then sign out of the app and sign back in.

The camera won't open when I scan.

Go to Settings → AFU on your iPhone and turn on Camera.

For Branch Presidents

How do I manage my branch?

New volunteers who join your branch are added to your roster automatically. From Team, you can create teams, add or remove members, and change officers.

How do I assign tasks?

Open the Task Board, create a task, add instructions, and choose who it's assigned to. Members see their tasks on their Home screen.

Account and privacy

To delete your account and all your data, go to Home → Account → Delete My Account. Deletion is permanent. To see how we handle your data, read our Privacy Policy.

AFU Volunteer Portal

Log your hours, record donations, and work with your branch, all in one app. Built for Advocacy for the Unhoused volunteers.

For every volunteer

Log hours

Record time from events and keep a running total on your Home screen.

Record donations

Scan a slip's barcode with your camera and log the amount in seconds.

Task Board

See your assigned tasks, read the instructions, and mark them done.

Reimbursements

Submit supply expenses with a receipt photo and track their status.

Notifications

Get alerts for new tasks, approvals, and branch updates.

Quick sign-in

Sign in with Apple or Google and join your branch right away.

For Branch Presidents

Start a branch

No AFU branch nearby? Set one up from the app and lead it.

Manage your roster

New members join your roster automatically.

Build teams

Create teams, add members, and assign officers.

Assign tasks

Post tasks with instructions and follow their progress.

About AFU

Advocacy for the Unhoused is a student-run nonprofit that supports people experiencing homelessness through donation drives, outreach, and community service. This app takes care of the paperwork so volunteers can spend their time helping.

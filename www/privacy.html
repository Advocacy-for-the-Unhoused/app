Privacy Policy

AFU Volunteer Portal · Advocacy for the Unhoused · Effective October 22, 2026

The AFU Volunteer Portal ("the app") is an internal tool for volunteers of Advocacy for the Unhoused (AFU), a student-run nonprofit. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address from Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, and the anonymous user ID Apple provides.
  • Registration details: your branch, an optional phone number, and an optional profile photo.
  • Roster details added by officers: your role and date of birth (used only to tell whether a parent or guardian must sign trip forms).
  • Volunteer activity: hours you log (event, date, hours, notes) and tasks assigned to you.
  • Donation records: the slip number (UDI) and dollar amount of cash donations you collected in person. The app never collects, processes, or transfers money, and stores nothing about donors.
  • Reimbursement requests: what you bought, the amount, a photo of the receipt, and your Venmo or Zelle handle if you choose to be paid that way.
  • Boston trip forms: your phone number, T-shirt size, acknowledgments, and typed signature. For volunteers under 18, a parent or guardian's name, email, phone number, and typed signature.
  • Device data: a push notification token so we can send you updates.

Camera, photos, and Face ID

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a donation slip or take a photo (profile photo or receipt). Slip scans are read on your device and only the slip number is kept. Photos are uploaded only when you submit them.

If you turn on Face ID or Touch ID sign-in, your biometric data never leaves your device and is never sent to us. We only keep your account email on your device so it can sign you back in.

How we use it

Only to run AFU's volunteer program: approving volunteers, tracking hours and donation records, organizing tasks and trips, reimbursing expenses, and sending notifications about these. We do not use your data for advertising or tracking, and we do not sell it.

Who can see it

AFU officers and branch leaders can see the information they need to manage volunteers — for example, your Branch President sees your registration and hours, and finance officers see reimbursement requests. Other volunteers can see your name on shared task boards.

Where it's stored and who processes it

  • Google (Google Sheets, Google Drive, Google Apps Script) stores app data in AFU's Google account. Uploaded photos and receipts are kept private, not shared by link.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) delivers push notifications.

We do not share your data with anyone else.

Young volunteers

The app is intended for volunteers 13 and older. Many volunteers are high school students, so trip forms for anyone under 18 require a parent or guardian's details and signature. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 has signed up, contact us and we will delete their account.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Home → Account → Delete My Account. Deletion is permanent and complete. We erase your roster entry, volunteer hours, Boston trip forms (including parent details and signature), reimbursement requests, any photos or receipts you uploaded, your date of birth, and your sign-in credentials. We also revoke Sign in with Apple access and delete your push notification tokens. No personal records are kept after deletion.

To see or correct your information before then, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and post the new version here and in the app.

Delete your AFU account

AFU Volunteer Portal (Android and iOS app "AFU") · Advocacy for the Unhoused

You can delete your AFU account and data at any time, either in the app or by emailing us. You can also ask us to delete only some of your information and keep your account.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open the AFU app and sign in.
  2. Go to Home → Account.
  3. Tap Delete My Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Option 2: Ask by email (no app needed)

Email president@hauhelps.org with the subject "Delete my AFU account". Send it from the email address you use to sign in to AFU, so we can confirm the account is yours. If you can't, tell us your name and branch and we'll help you verify.

We'll delete your account and reply to confirm within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deletion is permanent and complete. We erase:

  • Your roster entry (name, email, phone number, photo, date of birth)
  • Your volunteer hours
  • Your trip forms, including parent or guardian details and signatures
  • Your reimbursement requests and any photos or receipts you uploaded
  • Your sign-in credentials, including Sign in with Apple access
  • Your push notification tokens

No personal records are kept after deletion.

Delete only some of your data

You don't have to delete your whole account. To remove specific information, such as your phone number or profile photo, email president@hauhelps.org from your sign-in email and tell us what to delete. We'll remove it and reply to confirm within 30 days.

For more about what AFU collects and why, see our Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Advocacy for the Unhoused — Last updated June 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Advocacy for the Unhoused ("AFU", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the AFU App.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Your name and email address, obtained through Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In when you create an account.
  • Volunteer activity: Hours logged, tasks completed, expense reimbursement requests, and other volunteer records you submit through the app.
  • Device identifiers: A push notification token used solely to deliver app notifications to your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • To manage your volunteer account and track your contributions.
  • To send push notifications about tasks, meetings, and announcements.
  • To allow branch leadership to coordinate volunteer activities.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is accessible only to AFU staff and branch leadership within the app.

Data Storage

Volunteer data is stored in Google Sheets managed by Advocacy for the Unhoused. Authentication is handled entirely by Google or Apple — we never store your password.

Biometric Data

If you enable Face ID or Touch ID sign-in, biometric authentication is performed entirely on your device by iOS. AFU does not collect or store any biometric data.

Data Retention

Your volunteer records are retained for as long as you are an active volunteer with AFU. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

The AFU App is intended for volunteers aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at madhavsaxenaninja@gmail.com.
